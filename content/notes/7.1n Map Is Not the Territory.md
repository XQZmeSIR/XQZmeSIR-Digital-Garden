# Map Is Not the Territory

**Map Is Not the Territory** is the idea that models, representations, or theories are simplifications of reality and not reality itself.

## How to Use

- Remember that all models are approximations
- Be cautious about over-relying on any single model
- Useful in science, business, and planning

## Application

- Prevents dogmatic thinking
