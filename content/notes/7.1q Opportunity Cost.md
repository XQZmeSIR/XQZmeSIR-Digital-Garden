# Opportunity Cost

**Opportunity Cost** is the value of the next best alternative that you give up when making a decision. Every choice has a cost, even if it's not immediately obvious.

## How to Use
- Consider what you are sacrificing with each decision
- Weigh alternatives before committing
- Useful in time management and investing

## Application
- Helps prioritize actions and resources
