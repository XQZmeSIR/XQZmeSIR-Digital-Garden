# Latticework of Mental Models

A **latticework of mental models** is a collection of diverse frameworks from various disciplines (math, physics, psychology, economics, etc.) that together provide a robust toolkit for thinking and decision-making.

## Why Build a Latticework?
- Reduces blind spots
- Encourages multidisciplinary thinking
- Improves problem-solving

**Charlie Munger** popularized this approach. See [[notes/7.1a Charlie Munger]].
