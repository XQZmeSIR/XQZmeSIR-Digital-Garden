# Feedback Loops

**Feedback Loops** are processes where the outputs of a system are circled back and used as inputs, influencing future outputs. They can be positive (amplifying) or negative (stabilizing).

## How to Use

- Identify feedback mechanisms in systems
- Use feedback to adjust and improve
- Useful in learning, business, and engineering

## Application

- Essential for growth and stability
