# Inversion

**Inversion** is a mental model that involves thinking about a problem by considering the opposite or reverse scenario. Instead of asking, "How can I achieve success?" ask, "How could I fail?" and avoid those actions.

## How to Use
- Identify ways to fail or make mistakes
- Avoid those pitfalls
- Use for risk management and problem-solving

## Application
- Used by [[notes/7.1a Charlie Munger]]
- Helpful in troubleshooting and planning
