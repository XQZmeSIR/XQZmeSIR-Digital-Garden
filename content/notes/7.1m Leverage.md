# Leverage

**Leverage**, popularized by [[Naval Ravikant]] is the use of resources (money, time, skills, technology, or people) to achieve greater results than you could with your own effort alone.

## How to Use
- Identify points where small actions yield big results
- Use tools, systems, or people to amplify your impact
- Useful in business, investing, and productivity

## Application
- Maximizes efficiency and outcomes
