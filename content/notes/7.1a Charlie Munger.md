# Charlie Munger

**Charlie Munger** is the vice chairman of Berkshire Hathaway and a renowned advocate for using a broad set of mental models to improve decision-making. He believes in building a "latticework of mental models" from multiple disciplines to avoid blind spots and cognitive biases.

## Key Ideas
- Use a variety of mental models from different fields
- Avoid relying on a single perspective
- Regularly update and refine your models

## Famous Quote
> "All the wisdom of the world is not to be found in one little academic department."
