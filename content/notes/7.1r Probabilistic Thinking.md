# Probabilistic Thinking

**Probabilistic Thinking** is the practice of making decisions and predictions based on the likelihood of various outcomes, rather than certainty.

## How to Use
- Assign probabilities to possible outcomes
- Update beliefs as new information arrives
- Useful in risk management and forecasting

## Application
- Used in investing, science, and everyday decisions
