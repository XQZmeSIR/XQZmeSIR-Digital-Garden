# Circle of Competence

**Circle of Competence** is the idea that you should focus your efforts and decisions within areas where you have deep knowledge and experience, and avoid areas where you lack understanding.

## How to Use

- Know your strengths and limits
- Delegate or seek help outside your circle
- Regularly reassess your competence

## Application

- Used by [[7.1a Charlie Munger]] and Warren Buffett
- Helps avoid costly mistakes
