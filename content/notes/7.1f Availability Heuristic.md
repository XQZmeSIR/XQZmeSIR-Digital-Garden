# Availability Heuristic

**Availability Heuristic** is a mental shortcut where people judge the likelihood of events based on how easily examples come to mind, rather than actual probabilities.

## How to Use
- Be aware of recent or vivid events influencing your judgment
- Seek objective data
- Useful in risk assessment and decision-making

## Application
- Can lead to overestimating rare but memorable events
