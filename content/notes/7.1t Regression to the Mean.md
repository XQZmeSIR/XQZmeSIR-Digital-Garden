# Regression to the Mean

**Regression to the Mean** is the tendency for extreme outcomes to be followed by more typical ones, simply due to chance.

## How to Use

- Don't overreact to outliers
- Expect performance to average out over time
- Useful in performance evaluation and forecasting

## Application

- Common in sports, investing, and science
