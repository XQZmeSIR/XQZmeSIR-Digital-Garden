# Chesterton's Fence

**Chesterton's Fence** is the principle that you should not remove or change a system or rule until you understand why it was put in place.

## How to Use
- Investigate the original purpose of existing structures
- Avoid hasty changes without understanding consequences
- Useful in policy, business, and personal life

## Application
- Prevents unintended negative outcomes
