# Comparative Advantage

**Comparative Advantage** is the ability to produce a good or service at a lower opportunity cost than others, leading to specialization and trade.

## How to Use
- Focus on activities where you have a relative advantage
- Trade or collaborate for other needs
- Useful in economics, business, and personal productivity

## Application
- Drives specialization and efficiency
