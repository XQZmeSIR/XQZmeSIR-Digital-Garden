# Occam's Razor

**Occam's Razor** is the principle that, when faced with competing explanations or solutions, the simplest one is usually correct.

## How to Use
- Eliminate unnecessary assumptions
- Prefer straightforward solutions
- Use in troubleshooting and hypothesis testing

## Application
- Common in science, philosophy, and engineering
