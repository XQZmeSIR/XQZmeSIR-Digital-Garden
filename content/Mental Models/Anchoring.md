# Anchoring

**Anchoring** is the cognitive bias where people rely too heavily on the first piece of information (the "anchor") when making decisions.

## How to Use
- Be aware of initial values influencing your judgment
- Deliberately consider alternative perspectives
- Useful in negotiations, pricing, and forecasting

## Application
- Can lead to suboptimal decisions if unchecked
