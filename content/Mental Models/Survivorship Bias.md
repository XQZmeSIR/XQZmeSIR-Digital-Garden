# Survivorship Bias

**Survivorship Bias** is the error of focusing on successful people or things and ignoring those that failed, leading to false conclusions.

## How to Use
- Consider the full data set, not just the visible successes
- Avoid drawing conclusions from incomplete information

## Application
- Common in business, investing, and history
