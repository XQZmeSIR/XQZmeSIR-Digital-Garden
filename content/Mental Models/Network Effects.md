# Network Effects

**Network Effects** occur when a product or service becomes more valuable as more people use it.

## How to Use
- Build or join platforms that benefit from increased users
- Recognize compounding value in social, business, and technology networks

## Application
- Key to the success of companies like Facebook, Uber, and Airbnb
